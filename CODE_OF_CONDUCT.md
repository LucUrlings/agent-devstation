# Code of Conduct

We expect participants to be respectful and constructive. Harassment, discrimination, threats, and disclosure of another person's private information are not acceptable. Discuss technical disagreements on their merits and assume good faith.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately through GitHub's repository contact options; do not post sensitive reports publicly. Maintainers will review reports fairly and keep details limited to those handling them.
